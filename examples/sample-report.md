COVERAGE & METHOD
Unavailable source families: None among attempted queries.
Potentially capped query results: 2. Unscored due to language/cap: 10.
Duplicate articles are clustered; publisher count is not independent corroboration. Social metrics describe the collected sample only. Publication/discovery time may differ from event time.
Medium evidence confidence means a source page was read, with independent corroboration still open; Low means excerpt/social evidence only. No automated claim receives a verified label. Social author counts are unverified account labels; bot campaigns are not reliably detected.
A missing trend means insufficient comparable prior runs; no acceleration claim. Trends use broad discovery only, excluding adaptive follow-up. If no counterpoint appears, none was found in the sample; this is not proof none exists. Valuation, price reaction and what is already priced in remain unverified.

Method: local financial-text classifier plus explicit catalyst rules. Interpretations are hypotheses; social slang, sarcasm and multi-company stories can be misclassified.
